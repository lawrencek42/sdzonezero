Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using our services:

  • Identity information: First name, last name
  • Contact information: Email address, phone number (optional)
  • Property address (Sensitive Personal Information): Street address, city, state, ZIP code. Under California law, precise home addresses may be classified as Sensitive Personal Information. We collect your property address solely to provide the requested compliance assessment service and, with your separate consent, for anonymized community fire risk research.
  • Assessment data: Information you share during a professional walkthrough or inquiry, including property conditions and compliance questions
  • Consent record: Whether you agreed to our data research consent

We use essential functional cookies to manage your payment session and ensure the service functions correctly. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

We may also automatically collect limited technical data such as browser type and device type for service functionality purposes only.

// 02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your request and deliver your Zone Zero compliance service
  • Generate and send your personalized compliance report to the address you provide
  • Send you a copy of your results for your records
  • Respond to questions or support requests you send us
  • Notify you of relevant Zone Zero law updates or grant program developments that may affect your property (you may opt out at any time)
  • With your explicit consent, use anonymized and aggregated assessment data for wildfire safety research and community fire risk reporting

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling beyond generating your compliance assessment results.

// 03

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal contact information. We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Stripe: Payment processing for professional assessment services, operating under their own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy
  • Formspree: Form submission processing used to route contact and inquiry data to our internal systems
  • Google Workspace: Our business email and data storage platform, used to receive and retain your assessment data and correspondence
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority

All third-party service providers are engaged under terms that restrict their use of your data to providing services on our behalf. They may not use your data for their own marketing purposes.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your name, email, phone number, or property address to insurance companies, contractors, real estate agents, or any other third party for marketing purposes.

// 04

Payment Information

If you purchase a professional assessment service, all payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc., a PCI-compliant payment processor. Your payment card information is transmitted directly to Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

We do not have access to your full card number, CVV, or billing information beyond what you voluntarily provide in the contact form.

// 05

Assessment Data & Research Consent

During the service process, we ask for your optional consent to use your assessment findings — including your property address and compliance data — in anonymized, aggregated form for wildfire safety research and community fire risk reporting.

If you provide this consent:

  • Your data is anonymized before any research use — your name, email, and phone are never included
  • Aggregated data may be used to identify compliance trends by neighborhood or ZIP code
  • Your property address may appear in aggregate fire risk mapping without any personal identifiers attached
  • We may share anonymized findings with fire safety researchers, Fire Safe Councils, or public agencies

If you do not provide consent, your data is used solely to deliver your personal service and is not included in any research or aggregated reporting.

You may withdraw your research consent at any time by contacting us at info@sdzonezero.com.

// 06

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Specifically:

  • Assessment records and reports are retained for up to 3 years to allow you to reference your compliance history
  • Payment records are retained as required by financial regulations (typically 7 years)
  • Email communications are retained in our business email system for up to 2 years

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us (see Section 8).

// 07

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission on sdzonezero.com
  • Stripe’s PCI-compliant infrastructure for all payment processing
  • Google Workspace security standards for business email and data storage
  • Access to personal data limited to authorized personnel only

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing: Unsubscribe from any email communications using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by contacting us directly
  • Withdraw research consent: Opt out of anonymized data research at any time by contacting us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@sdzonezero.com. We will respond within 30 days.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

As a California business serving California residents, we comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of personal information we have collected
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell personal information
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: You have the right to limit our use of your sensitive personal information (including your property address) to only what is necessary to perform our services. We do not use your property address for any purpose other than generating your compliance report and, with your separate explicit consent, for anonymized community fire risk research.

To submit a CCPA/CPRA request, contact us at info@sdzonezero.com with the subject line “CCPA Request.” We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days as required by law.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@sdzonezero.com.
