The Challenge

Most Homeowners Feel Completely Lost.

Zone Zero requirements are real, the deadline is firm, and the official guidance is scattered across government websites written in technical language. Here's what we hear every day:

📋

“I don't even know what Zone Zero means for my specific property.”

The rules vary by property type, materials, and location. Checklists don't cut it — you need a property-specific assessment.

🏠

“My insurance company is already asking questions.”

Insurers are increasingly scrutinizing fire zone properties. Documented compliance isn't just about the city deadline — it can protect your coverage right now.

🔨

“I don't know where to even begin with the changes.”

Zone Zero involves your landscaping, fencing, vents, gutters, and more. Without a clear starting point, most homeowners do nothing — and that's the most expensive mistake of all.

📅

“February 2027 feels far away — but is it?”

Some changes could require permits, contractor scheduling, and material lead times. Waiting longer to start may mean missing the deadline entirely.

Why Lawrence Started This
“I live in the Very High Fire Severity Zone. My backyard sits on the slope of Highway 52 and Regents Road. This isn't abstract for me — it's my home too.”

When San Diego adopted Zone Zero, I watched my neighbors struggle to find real answers. The official guidance is a starting point, but it doesn't tell you what to actually do at your specific property. The answers are out there, but buried. Most homeowners don't have the time or resources to find them.

San Diego Zone Zero Solutions exists because your neighbors need a trusted local guide, not another confusing document. I've already helped homeowners in Poway — walking their property, identifying potential risks, and delivering something they could actually use: a clear record of the progress they'd made. Something to reference later, show their insurer, and contractors. San Diego officials aren't demanding perfection. They want to see that you're making progress — and we help you document every step of it.

— Lawrence, Founder · University City, San Diego
